Capacity, safety, and code concerns made clearer.

This group helps customers request provider review for aging panels, insufficient capacity, insurance concerns, remodels, appliance additions, and visible electrical defects.

Detailed services

For homes and buildings that need more than a quick repair.

Panel work should be scoped carefully around service size, grounding, permits, utility coordination, and future load demand.

  • Panel replacement and upgrades
    Old panels, insufficient breaker spaces, damaged equipment, subpanel additions, and service capacity requests.
  • Whole-home surge protection
    Surge device installation requests, grounding review, and sensitive equipment protection planning.
  • Safety inspections
    Visible defects, outdated devices, grounding concerns, open junctions, overloaded circuits, and buyer or seller checklists.
  • Code corrections
    Issues flagged by inspectors, insurance, renovation teams, or property management requirements.
