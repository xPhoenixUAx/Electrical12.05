How requests move

Structured intake before a provider is assigned.

VoltWise is built to make electrical requests specific enough for the right provider to respond with fewer surprises.

  • Issue classification
    We separate troubleshooting, installation, inspection, upgrade, and urgent safety scenarios.
  • Local routing
    Requests are directed around service area, property type, and availability.
  • Scope notes
    The request captures panel details, symptoms, access, photos, and preferred timing.
  • Provider confirmation
    Final quote, materials, permits, and schedule are confirmed by the assigned provider.
Request workflow

From vague issue to workable handoff.

The process is designed to gather practical details before a provider accepts the request: symptoms, photos, panel access, timing, and service group.

Structured intake reduces back-and-forth before dispatch.
01

Describe the issue

Choose a service group and share symptoms, location, urgency, photos, and whether anything feels unsafe.

 02

Route the request

The request is classified by property type, work category, access needs, and likely provider fit.

 03
