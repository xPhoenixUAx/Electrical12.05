This Cookie Policy explains how ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and related electrical service coordination pages (the "Service"). This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can be session cookies, persistent cookies, first-party cookies, or third-party cookies. Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. First-party cookies are set by the website you are visiting. Third-party cookies are set by another domain.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, accessibility, form behavior, and session integrity. You cannot opt out of essential cookies.

Examples may include cookie consent preferences and session management while browsing.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information in an aggregated or anonymous manner. This helps us improve website performance, page structure, request flow, and user experience.

Examples may include website traffic, page views, user behavior patterns, and performance monitoring.

3.3 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization. They may remember preferences or temporarily support form-related features while you browse the Service.

Examples may include language preferences or temporary form information.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to help measure advertising performance and understand engagement with marketing campaigns. Where required by law, optional marketing cookies should be subject to consent settings.

Examples may include advertising network measurement and social media-related tracking.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies on your device, such as analytics, advertising, performance, or social media services. These third parties may use cookies to collect information about your online activities across different websites. We do not control third-party cookies, and you should review the privacy and cookie policies of the relevant third-party providers.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to accept or reject non-essential cookies where applicable. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline or delete cookies.

5.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Popular browsers such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge provide cookie management controls in their privacy or security settings.

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

You may also be able to opt out of certain third-party cookies through tools provided by analytics providers, advertising industry groups, or browser-level privacy controls.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on our website. Some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is currently no consistent standard for how DNT signals should be interpreted, our website does not currently respond to DNT browser signals or mechanisms.
