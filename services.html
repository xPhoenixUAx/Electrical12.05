Service groups

Five clear categories, each with its own detail page.

Every category is organized around real service conversations: what happened, what equipment is involved, what access is needed, and what the provider may need to verify.

Residential electrical

For everyday electrical issues in houses, condos, apartments, kitchens, bathrooms, garages, and home offices.

  • Outlet, switch, GFCI, and AFCI troubleshooting
  • Lighting, dimmers, ceiling fans, and fixture boxes
  • Dedicated circuits for appliances, laundry, pumps, and workshops

Commercial systems

For offices, retail spaces, restaurants, light facilities, landlords, and property managers who need controlled scheduling and clean documentation.

  • LED retrofits, emergency lighting, sensors, and exterior lights
  • Conduit, equipment circuits, tenant improvements, and maintenance

Emergency response

For urgent electrical symptoms that need professional review without turning the page into fear-based marketing.

  • Repeated breaker trips, buzzing, heat, or unusual smells
  • Partial power loss, storm-related symptoms, and exterior equipment concerns
  • Essential circuit issues where safe isolation may be needed
