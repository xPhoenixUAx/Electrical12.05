Commercial scope

Built around uptime and code-conscious execution.

Typical customers include office managers, retail operators, landlords, facility coordinators, restaurants, and light industrial spaces.

  • Distribution panels and breakers
    Panel troubleshooting, labeling, breaker replacement, load review, subpanel planning, and service-room corrections.
  • Lighting systems
    LED retrofits, emergency lighting, exterior security lighting, occupancy sensors, track lighting, and fixture replacement.
  • Conduit and dedicated circuits
    Surface conduit, equipment circuits, POS stations, server closets, appliances, shop tools, and controlled routing.
  • Tenant improvement support
    Coordination for build-outs, device placement, fixture plans, inspection corrections, and contractor sequencing.
  • Maintenance visits
    Scheduled inspection, nuisance trip review, thermal symptoms, loose device replacement, and documentation.
