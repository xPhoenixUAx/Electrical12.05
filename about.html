About the network

Electrical requests need a better first handoff.

VoltWise is a coordination layer for homeowners and property teams who need electrical help but do not always know which provider category, project details, or safety notes matter first.

How the network thinks

A practical aggregator, not a contractor costume.

Electrical requests become useful when the first handoff captures the property, symptoms, equipment, urgency, and access details a provider actually needs.

Electrical projects often fail at the first conversation. A customer says "the power is weird," but the provider needs to know what room, what breaker, what changed, whether there is heat or smell, whether the panel is accessible, and whether the work is urgent.

VoltWise turns that ambiguity into a structured request. The assigned provider still confirms pricing, code requirements, permits, schedule, materials, and warranty details, but the first handoff becomes cleaner and more useful.

Trust-first languageNo fake "number one" claims, no panic copy, and no promises a local provider must confirm.
Structured service groupsResidential, commercial, emergency, EV, and panel work are treated as distinct workflows.
Clear aggregator roleThe site states the coordination role clearly and keeps legal details configurable.
