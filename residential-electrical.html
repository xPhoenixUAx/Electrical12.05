Residential electrical

Electrical work for safer, more usable homes.

VoltWise routes home electrical requests to providers suited for troubleshooting, installations, fixture work, circuit additions, and practical safety upgrades.

Request residential helpAll service groups
Services included

From small fixes to planned upgrades.

Residential service requests are grouped by what a homeowner is trying to solve: comfort, safety, reliability, or new equipment power.

  • Outlet and switch repair
    Loose outlets, dead receptacles, damaged plates, warm switches, GFCI/AFCI issues, and room-by-room replacement.
  • Lighting and fixture installation
    Recessed lighting, pendant fixtures, exterior lights, dimmers, landscape lighting circuits, and safer fixture boxes.
  • Ceiling fans and ventilation
    Fan-rated boxes, wall controls, bathroom exhaust fans, and wiring corrections for overloaded switch legs.
  • Dedicated circuits
    Appliance circuits for ranges, microwaves, laundry equipment, sump pumps, workshops, and home offices.
  • Home safety corrections
    Grounding checks, GFCI protection, visible wiring defects, old device replacement, and practical hazard review.
Methods and tools

Real tools, careful diagnostics.

Providers commonly use standard meters and code-aware methods before replacing parts.

Voltage testing

Multimeters, receptacle testers, and non-contact verification where appropriate.

Device replacement

Proper torque, box fill review, grounding continuity, and compatible switches or outlets.

Scope notes

Room location, panel access, photos, fixture type, and symptom history.
